About Autism Spectrum Disorder

Understanding ASD — the facts, signs, and support available.

🔬

What is Autism?

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a complex neurodevelopmental condition that affects how a person communicates, behaves, and interacts with others. It is called a "spectrum" because symptoms and severity vary widely across individuals.

📊

How Common is it?

According to the CDC, approximately 1 in 36 children in the United States is diagnosed with ASD. It occurs in all racial, ethnic, and socioeconomic groups. It is about 4 times more common in boys than in girls.

⚠️

Early Signs

  • Delayed speech or language skills
  • Avoiding eye contact
  • Difficulty understanding others feelings
  • Repeating words or phrases (echolalia)
  • Intense interest in specific topics
  • Sensitivity to sensory input (light, sound, texture)
🧬

Causes

The exact cause of ASD is not fully understood. Research suggests it involves a combination of genetic and environmental factors. It is not caused by vaccines — this has been thoroughly studied and disproven.

💊

Treatment & Support

There is no single cure for autism, but many therapies help individuals lead fulfilling lives. These include Applied Behaviour Analysis (ABA), speech therapy, occupational therapy, and social skills training. Early intervention is key.

❤️

Living with Autism

Many autistic individuals lead independent, successful, and meaningful lives. With the right support, people on the spectrum excel in fields like science, art, music, and technology. Autism is a difference, not a deficiency.
