🎥 Facial Behaviour Analysis

Real-time webcam analysis of behavioral indicators associated with autism screening.

📊 What We Analyze

👁️

Eye Contact

Measures how consistently you maintain gaze toward the screen. Reduced eye contact is a key ASD indicator.

😑

Blink Rate

Counts blinks per minute. Normal range is 12–22 blinks/min. Atypical rates may indicate ASD.

🔄

Head Movement

Tracks head stability. Repetitive or excessive head movement is a behavioral ASD indicator.

😊

Expression

Measures facial expressiveness via eye variability. Flat affect is a common ASD feature.

👤

Face Presence

Tracks how long the face remains visible and engaged during the session.

🤖

Combined Score

Weighted combination of all 5 indicators into one behavioral risk score.

📋 Before You Start

  • ✅ Sit in a well-lit room facing your webcam directly
  • ✅ Keep your face centered and fully visible throughout
  • ✅ Behave completely naturally — do not force expressions
  • ✅ A webcam window will open with your face mesh overlay
  • ✅ The window closes automatically when analysis is complete
  • ⚠️ Press Q on the webcam window only if you need to stop early
  • ⚠️ Do not close the browser tab while analysis is running

💡 What This Means

📊 Score Breakdown Explanation

70–100 — Low Risk

Eye contact, blink rate, and head movement are all within typical ranges.

45–69 — Moderate Risk

Some indicators are slightly outside typical ranges. May be due to lighting, anxiety, or camera angle.

0–44 — High Risk

Multiple indicators show atypical patterns. Recommend completing AQ-10 and consulting a professional.
